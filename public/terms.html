Music is art, heart, and dedication. We deeply honor copyright owners, artists, and producers:

  • Personal Streaming License: You are granted a personal, non-commercial right to stream and enjoy all available tracks on JamiiSound.
  • Creator Ownership: Songs, artwork, and lyrics remain the intellectual property of their respective creators and distributors.
  • Fair Community Conduct: We ask all community members to enjoy music responsibly and avoid unauthorized commercial resale or automated mass extraction.

3 Your Account & Responsibilities

Creating an account unlocks custom playlists, favorites, and synchronized listening. We ask you to:

  • Provide accurate registration information and keep your login password secure.

  • Feel free to update your preferences or close your account at any time with full dignity and ease.
