Privacy Policy

User Privacy, Trust & Data Protection Commitment

Built on Trust, Empowerment & Mutual Respect

Welcome to JamiiSound. We believe music is a universal cultural bridge, and every listener and artist deserves an inspiring, respectful, and safe digital home. Your personal information belongs to you. This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent, user-first approach to keeping your account secure and delivering the best sound experience possible.

1 Information We Collect

We only collect the essential information required to give you an uninterrupted, high-fidelity music streaming experience:

Account & Identity
Your display name, avatar, and email address used for secure sign-in and personalized account settings.
Musical Taste & Library
Your favorite songs, saved playlists, play queues, and sound equalizer preferences so you can pick up where you left off.

2 How We Use Your Data

Everything we do with your data is designed to elevate your personal music journey:

  • Seamless Playback: Syncing your playlists, queues, and listening history smoothly across your devices.
  • Tailored Discovery: Recommending trending tracks, emerging African and international artists, and curated charts.
  • Audio Perfection: Saving your personalized 3-band equalizer profiles and lyrics synchronization preferences.
  • Account Protection: Providing instant security alerts, password resets, and reliable account recovery.

Zero Data Selling & Full Privacy Respect

We value your trust above all else:

  • We never sell your data: JamiiSound does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or listening habits to third-party data brokers or marketing networks.
  • Industry-Standard Security: We utilize modern cloud encryption, secure HTTPS streaming protocols, and role-based access to safeguard your account.

3 You Are in Complete Control

You hold complete ownership of your JamiiSound experience. At any time, you have the right to:

  • Update or modify your profile name, email, and password.
  • Create, modify, or delete any of your playlists and saved favorites.
  • Request a copy of your account data or request permanent deletion of your profile and listening records.
